# Changelog

All notable changes to EnemyHUD. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [1.3.0] - 2026-11-01
### Added
- `//ehud hide` and `//ehud show`, e.g. to clear the screen while reading the map (not saved).

## [1.2.1] - 2026-11-01
### Fixed
- Buff and debuff icons now follow `icon_size`; they were always drawn at the PNG's 32x32.
- Sleep (including Lullaby) is cleared when the monster takes damage or acts. The game sends no
  wear-off message when damage wakes a monster, so the icon used to stay up.

## [1.2.0] - 2026-10-31
### Added
- Monster buffs in their own row above the debuffs; icons sit on green (buff) or red (debuff) tiles.
- Dispel, Erase and steal messages remove the effect from either row.
- `icon_size` setting (default 14).
### Changed
- Party members' pets are no longer treated as monsters, so avatar blood pact debuffs are tracked.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-31
### Added
- Debuffs from job abilities, blood pacts, weapon skills, TP moves, dances, runes and added effects.
### Changed
- Rounded panel with a gold rim and pill-shaped bars (generated by `tools/enemyhud_textures.py`).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-31
### Added
- First version: HP bar with damage trail, claim-colored name, level from a quiet `/check`,
  Targeting row, cast/readying bar, debuff icons with timers. Replaces the official enemybar.
